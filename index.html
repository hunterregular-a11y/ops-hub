A three-minute guide.

Start: Name your project. Record the goal, current state, and next step. Add decisions and boundaries when they matter.

Resume: Check the generated brief, copy it, and paste it into any AI chat. OpsHub does not connect to your AI account.

Update: After working, save your current state and next step with “Update after a session.” A dated checkpoint keeps the earlier version recoverable.

Check: Paste an AI’s actual plan. Read any flagged actions. The checker matches English patterns and excluded terms. It can miss or misunderstand actions. Your review is the decision.

Protect your notes: Export a backup regularly. Import it when moving to a new browser or device. Browser saves are not encrypted, synced, or permanent backups.

Phone tip: Use the five tabs at the bottom and the project selector at the top. Local HTML file support varies by browser. If saving is unavailable, OpsHub shows a warning and lets you export your working copy.

Cost: OpsHub itself makes no AI calls and loads no third-party assets. It does not verify the terms or cost of your AI service.
